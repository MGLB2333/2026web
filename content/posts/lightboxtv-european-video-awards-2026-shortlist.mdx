We're delighted to announce that LightBoxTV has been shortlisted in two categories at the 2026 European Video Awards:

- **Best CTV Ad Tech Innovation** for the LightBoxTV platform
- **Best New Product**, alongside our partners at dentsu

The nominations recognise our work to modernise connected TV advertising. LightBoxTV brings planning, trading and campaign delivery into one connected workflow, replacing processes traditionally reliant on spreadsheets, manual administration and disconnected systems.

The Best New Product nomination is also a fantastic acknowledgement of our partnership with dentsu, combining its industry expertise with LightBoxTV's technology to make TV planning and buying simpler and more effective.

We're proud to be recognised alongside such an impressive group of innovators and look forward to celebrating with all the finalists at the awards ceremony in London on 1 October 2026.
